Terms and Conditions

Draft terms for the AttendWise project. Review and adapt before public launch.

Purpose

AttendWise is a personal attendance and timetable management tool. It is not an official college attendance system and does not verify attendance with an institution.

Accuracy and attendance calculations

Calculations depend on the records, targets, timetable, and class statuses entered by the user. Institutional attendance policies may use different rules. Verify official attendance with your institution before making academic decisions.

Accounts and security

Keep account credentials confidential and provide accurate information. Do not attempt to access another person's data, disrupt the service, or misuse notification functionality.

Notifications

Reminders are best-effort. AttendWise cannot guarantee delivery at an exact time because browsers, devices, permissions, networks, and push services can delay or block notifications. Keep your own timetable and check institutional announcements.

Availability and changes

The service may change or become unavailable during maintenance. This project is provided as-is for learning and evaluation until the operator publishes applicable service commitments.

Limitation

To the extent permitted by applicable law, the operator is not responsible for academic decisions made solely from app calculations or missed reminders. Nothing in these terms excludes liability that cannot legally be excluded.
