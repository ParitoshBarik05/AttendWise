Privacy Policy

Draft policy for the AttendWise project. Review and adapt before public launch.

Information we collect

AttendWise stores the name and email address you provide, a password hash, subjects, timetable entries, attendance records, notification preferences, and push subscription details when you enable reminders.

How information is used

We use this information to authenticate your account, calculate attendance, show timetable history, and deliver class reminders you enable. Attendance calculations are based on records you enter and are not an official institutional record.

Storage and sharing

Data is stored in the database configured by the operator. The operator should not sell personal data. Hosting, database, and push delivery providers may process data to operate the service. Review provider terms before launch.

Security and retention

Passwords must be stored as hashes, secrets must remain server-side, and access must be restricted to the authenticated account. You may delete subjects and their related records from the app. Account deletion and data export should be implemented before a public production launch.

Notifications

Push notifications are optional and require browser/device permission. You can disable them in browser settings and remove the subscription from the app where that control is available. Delivery depends on device, browser, connectivity, and platform behavior.

Children and contact

AttendWise is intended for users who are permitted to use the service under applicable law and institutional rules. Replace this paragraph with the operator's real contact details and applicable legal requirements before publishing.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the service changes. The operator should publish an effective date and a contact address before public launch.
